# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, be constructive, assume good faith. Harassment, personal attacks, and
discriminatory language are not tolerated in any project space (issues, PRs, discussions).

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers at the contact address in the
repository metadata. Reports are reviewed confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject
contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior that violates this
code.
